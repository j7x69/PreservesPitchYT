# Privacy Policy for PreservesPitch YT

**Effective Date:** October 19, 2026

PreservesPitch YT ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how your data is handled.

**1. Data Collection and Usage**
The Extension does not collect, transmit, distribute, or sell any personal data, browser history, or analytics. 

**2. Storage Permission**
The Extension uses the `storage` permission solely to save your visual preference (whether the Cassette Effect is toggled ON or OFF). This single boolean value is saved locally using the `chrome.storage.sync` API, which syncs directly with your Google account. It is never transmitted to the developer or any third-party servers.

**3. Contact**
If you have any questions or concerns regarding this policy, please open an issue on the Extension's GitHub repository.
